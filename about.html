About Me

This is what I do.

I am a creative and motivated individual with a strong passion for art, design, and marketing. I enjoy expressing ideas through visual storytelling and bringing creativity into both personal projects and professional work. With a focus on marketing, I am driven by opportunities to connect with others, communicate meaningful ideas, and create positive impact.

I am the founder of Ink & Parcel, a commissioned art business where I create customized pieces tailored to individual stories and experiences. This venture has strengthened my creativity, attention to detail, and ability to connect personally with clients through my work.

I served a mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Taipei, Taiwan, where I spent a year and a half developing fluency in Mandarin Chinese and building meaningful relationships with people from diverse backgrounds. This experience deepened my appreciation for global connection, cultural understanding, and service-oriented leadership.

Previously, I studied Interior Design and Architecture at Mount Royal University for a year and a half before transferring to Brigham Young University, where I am currently pursuing studies in the Marriott School of Business. Through my academic and personal experiences, I have developed a strong foundation in creativity, communication, and problem-solving.

Above all, I am passionate about uplifting others and aspire to use my skills in marketing, creativity, and communication to make a meaningful and lasting impact in the lives of those around me.
